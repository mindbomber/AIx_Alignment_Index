# Changelog

## 0.1.0 - 2026-06-12

Initial research-alpha MVP:

- complete five-domain, 29-indicator AIx instrument specification
- evidence-weighted arithmetic and geometric scoring
- configurable PCP, LVP, HEP, and LEP penalties
- skew, balance, domain floors, score bands, profile distance, and ATS dynamics proxy
- Markdown, JSON, CSV, and HTML reports
- validation, scoring, reporting, comparison, batch, export, rubric, reliability, and chart CLI
- quadratic weighted kappa, ICC(2,1), Cronbach alpha, and disagreement diagnostics
- PNG/SVG domain, radar, penalty, skew, and before/after charts
- four assessments including the Expanded Edition worked example
- executable notebook, tests, CI, documentation, and release tooling
