# Cross-Domain Use Cases

## AI Systems

Evaluate factuality, numerical validity, feasibility, safety, cognitive burden,
instruction coherence, hidden assumptions, distribution shift, auditability,
calibration, abstention, and correction.

## Digital Platforms

Detect profiles where engagement or revenue objectives score highly while wellbeing,
trust, factual environment, hidden systemic risk, and independent feedback deteriorate.

## Institutions

Diagnose compliance success under mission failure: procedures and throughput remain
strong while material feasibility, service quality, dignity, or correction pathways drift.

## Markets and Economies

Assess constructed coherence alongside ecological limits, externalized costs, labor
wellbeing, opacity, tail risk, and corrective governance.

## Healthcare

Prioritize clinical and factual validity, patient safety, dignity, access, uncertainty,
auditability, appeal, and correction latency. Consequential use requires empirically
calibrated higher domain floors.

## Legal Systems

Assess factual grounding, jurisdiction, procedural legitimacy, proportionality, due
process, hidden bias, social trust, enforcement externalities, and appeal mechanisms.

## Education

Test whether metrics such as completion rates or test scores remain connected to
learning, cognitive sustainability, accessibility, agency, and corrective feedback.

## Individual Decisions

Use AIx as a structured reflection tool for material feasibility, wellbeing, obligations,
unknown risks, and feedback loops. It does not replace professional advice.
